# Winning-ad remake workflow

1. Find the reference ad with `TrendTrack.search_ads(...)`, then fetch it with `TrendTrack.get_ad(...)` and `TrendTrack.download_creative(...)`. If TrendTrack is unavailable or has no credible result, use the relevant public ad-library search and download calls. Never publish or promote the competitor ad.
2. Score evidence such as run length, active status, variants, placements, engagement, and repeated creative patterns. Label every subjective judgment as **opinion** and every inferred number as **estimate**. Reject prior copies, weak product/audience fits, insufficient evidence, and remakes that would look obviously AI-generated.
3. Analyze video references with the available media inspection/transcription calls. Produce and link: exact cut list with timestamps, full frame sheet, first-three-second frame sheet, per-shot silent clips, literal shot notes, transcript, pacing notes, voice notes, and music notes. For stills, map composition using percentage-based x/y zones.
4. Draft a remake plan that closely preserves hook, sequence, framing, pacing, transitions, text timing, and format while replacing all competitor products, branding, people, music, and voices. Use only supported claims, 2–4 real product photos, and two clearly different-person casting options. Never fabricate reviews, testimonials, scarcity, endorsements, results, or product capabilities.
5. Default to Seedance 2.5 for video, the latest GPT Image model for stills, and the copied source format. Before every paid generation batch, show: exact units, per-unit price, subtotal, 20% retry margin, total estimated cost, currency, and available balance. Label uncertain pricing as an estimate and wait for explicit approval before calling any paid generation tool.
6. Generate only the approved batch. Preserve real product appearance from the supplied photos; reject distorted packaging, unreadable labels, implausible anatomy or motion, identity leakage, and generally unconvincing AI output. Do not silently spend retries: present another batch estimate and obtain approval first.
7. Compare source and remake shot by shot. Score structural faithfulness and production quality with brief reasons. Require faithfulness ≥8/10 and quality ≥7/10, and fail immediately for any competitor name, logo, packaging, product, person, voice, music, watermark, caption, metadata, or other trace. Revise only through an approved paid batch.
8. Deliver every created file as a clickable link, including analysis artifacts, prompts, source-photo selections, intermediate renders, finals, and comparison sheets. While awaiting a decision, offer explicit click options such as **Approve batch**, **Choose person A**, **Choose person B**, **Request changes**, **Approve final**, and **Stop**.
9. After final approval, prepare the campaign with default budget `20/day` in local currency and name it `competitor · angle · format · date`. Keep every Meta campaign, ad set, and ad paused; call the relevant Meta read/status tool after creation or edits to verify each remains paused. Never publish, activate, schedule spend, or spend money without separate explicit approval.
10. Once the user approves the final remake and confirms the competitor reference is no longer needed, delete locally stored competitor creative files with the available file-delete call. Do not delete analysis artifacts or user/product assets unless separately requested.

Work is done only when all requested files are linked, the final passes both score thresholds with zero competitor traces, paid actions have matching approvals, any Meta items are re-checked as paused, and confirmed competitor source files are deleted.
